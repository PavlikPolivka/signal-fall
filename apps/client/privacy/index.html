Privacy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Signal Fall is a free browser game. It collects as little as it can, and nothing for advertising.

What is stored

  • A player account. Everyone starts as an anonymous guest. The account has an id and a display name you can change.
  • Your email, only if you choose to keep your account with an email link, or with Google or GitHub (we receive the email address and name those services share; nothing else, and no access to your files or repositories).
  • Match records: factions, opponent, result, length, map seed and a short score summary, for your history, records and the leaderboard. Rated matches and your rating are visible to other players, with your display name.
  • Desync reports: if an online match goes out of sync, the game sends the match's command log so we can fix the bug.
  • In your browser only: your settings, last skirmish setup and the session that keeps you signed in.

What is not

No ads, no analytics or tracking cookies, no selling or sharing of data. Online matches connect players directly to each other; the game does not record your chat or voice (there is none) or your IP address beyond what the hosting services log for security.

Who processes it

  • Supabase (database and sign-in, hosted in Frankfurt, EU).
  • Vercel (serves the website).
  • Resend (sends the sign-in and confirmation emails).
  • Cloudflare (relays online-match traffic when players cannot connect directly, and the domain's DNS).

Your choices

You can play without an account (as a guest) and without giving an email. To see, export or delete your account and match records, email privacy@signal-fall.app from the address on your account, or write from any address with your display name. Deletion removes your profile, email and match records.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
