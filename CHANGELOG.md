# What's new

## 2026-10-18 23:28 UTC

- **Doctrines:** each faction's two special upgrades at the tech center are now a choice: research one and the other closes for the match. Holdout picks Salvage Crews or Reinforced Plating, Halcyon Extended Relays or Deep Recompile, the Choir Harmonic Shields or Rapid Growth. They show with a dashed border, and the one you didn't pick is crossed out.

## 2026-10-18 23:13 UTC

- **Touch screens:** play on a tablet. Tap to select or give orders, drag one finger to move the view, pinch with two fingers to zoom, and press and hold to back out (like a right click). A touch bar appears with **Box** (drag to select a group), **Army**, **Attack**, **Stop** and **Cancel**.

## 2026-10-18 23:10 UTC

- **Survival:** a new skirmish mode. Build up and hold out against 15 waves that come from the map edges, bigger every time, with heavier units as they go. Play alone or with an AI ally, on Easy, Normal or Hard waves. The top bar counts the waves and the time to the next.
- **Co-op survival** in multiplayer rooms: you and a friend (or two friends, or friends and AI) on one side against the waves.

## 2026-10-18 22:48 UTC

- **Map editor:** **Map editor** on the main menu. Paint ground, rough, rock, cliffs, water and city ruins, lay Echo fields and place the starts, with mirroring as you paint so every side gets the same land. **Check** tells you if it's fair and playable, **Play vs AI** tries it out, and every map has a code you can share; paste a code to load someone's map.
- **Hand-made maps:** four designed maps ship with the game: **Crossroads**, **Twin Rivers**, **Fortress** (1v1) and **Four Corners** (four players). Pick them, or your own saved maps, under **Map** in the skirmish setup or a multiplayer room.

## 2026-10-18 22:37 UTC

- **Tutorial:** new to real-time strategy? **Tutorial** on the main menu teaches the game in eight short steps on a small map: moving the camera, setting up a base, power, money, an army, control groups, attacking and winning. Each step ticks off as soon as you've done it.

## 2026-10-18 22:33 UTC

- **Daily challenge:** every day there is one skirmish that's the same for everyone: your faction, the opponent (Hard AI) and the map follow from the date. Win it as fast as you can; the main menu and the end screen show today's fastest wins. Only your best time counts.

## 2026-10-18 22:29 UTC

- **King of the hill:** set **Victory** to King of the hill in the map options. A flag stands at the center of the map; hold it (ground units next to it, no enemy beside them) for five minutes in total and you win. The top bar shows how long each side has held it. Destroying the enemy still wins too. The AI fights for the hill.

## 2026-10-18 22:11 UTC

- **Free-for-all:** play against two or three AIs at once, everyone for themselves, in skirmish (Mode: Free-for-all 3 or 4), or with friends in a multiplayer room. The last side standing wins.

## 2026-10-18 22:07 UTC

- **Crates:** a new map option. With **Crates** on, a supply crate drops somewhere on the map every 45 seconds. The first ground unit to reach one opens it: 500 credits, a full repair for your units around it, or a promotion for the unit that found it. The AI grabs the ones it sees, too.

## 2026-10-18 21:57 UTC

- **No false "disconnected":** a short network or computer hiccup in multiplayer no longer leaves the "Opponent disconnected" screen up for good. It goes away as soon as the other player is heard from again.

## 2026-10-18 21:24 UTC

- **Capture points:** a new map option. Turn on **Capture points** in the skirmish or lobby map settings, and the land between the bases holds sites to fight over: an **Echo derrick** that pumps credits, a **watchtower** that sees far, and a **repair depot** that mends your units. Keep ground units next to one, with no enemy beside them, for 10 seconds to take it. The AI goes for the ones on its side of the map. Each site has its own 3D model, and they show on the minimap.

## 2026-10-18 19:49 UTC

- **Ranked match:** press **Ranked match** on the Multiplayer screen to be paired with someone near your rating. The room is made for you and the match starts as soon as you're both connected. It counts for the leaderboard.

## 2026-10-18 19:47 UTC

- **Reload and carry on:** if you refresh the page (or the browser restarts the tab) during a multiplayer match, you rejoin it automatically: the game reconnects to the other players, catches up on everything that happened, and puts you back in your seat, in step with everyone.

## 2026-10-18 19:06 UTC

- **Watch your friends play:** enter a room code and press **Watch** instead of Join. You see the whole match live, with no fog, without taking a seat. Up to four people can watch, and the players never wait for them.

## 2026-10-18 19:01 UTC

- **Open games:** hosts can tick **List in Open games** in their room, and anyone can find it on the Multiplayer screen and join with one click: no code needed. The list shows the mode and the free seats.

## 2026-10-18 18:57 UTC

- **Rematch in multiplayer:** **Rematch** on the end screen takes everyone back to the same room, with the same map, seats and factions. The host starts the next match when all are back.
- **When someone leaves:** if a player disconnects, **Let the AI take over** keeps the match going with the AI playing their side. Everyone left agrees on the exact moment, so nobody goes out of sync, and replays show the takeover too.

## 2026-10-18 18:49 UTC

- **Chat in multiplayer:** press <kbd>Enter</kbd>, type, <kbd>Enter</kbd> to send. In 2v2, a button picks whether your team or everyone reads it.
- **Pings:** <kbd>Alt</kbd>+click the battlefield to mark a spot for your team: rings in your color on their screen and minimap, with a chirp.
- **Faction sounds:** interface clicks and cues are pitched for your faction.

## 2026-10-18 18:43 UTC

- **Formation move:** with several units selected, right-drag a line on the ground; they spread out evenly along it, each taking the spot nearest its place in line.
- **Rebind keys:** **Keys** in the menu lists every command; click one and press its new key (Ctrl, Shift and Alt combinations work). Clashes are refused, and **Reset to defaults** undoes it all. The Codex shows your keys.

## 2026-10-18 18:40 UTC

- **Faction music:** the score plays in your faction's style: a gritty minor march for the Holdout, cold exact tones for Halcyon, slow unresolved chords for the Choir. It still swells with the fighting.
- **Base ambience:** power plants on screen hum quietly, and your factories clank now and then.

## 2026-10-18 18:38 UTC

- **Opponent style:** pick who you play against in the skirmish setup: Steady Sam (balanced), Rusher Rita (early pressure), Tech Tara (heavy units later), Turtle Tomas (defenses, then one big push) or Raider Rex (fast units after your harvesters). Random still picks a plan each match.
- **Know your counters:** every unit's card in the sidebar lists the enemy units that beat it.

## 2026-10-18 18:34 UTC

- **Dusk and night:** pick the time of day in the skirmish setup. At night the battlefield goes dark blue, buildings throw warm pools of light, units carry lamps and Echo glows cyan. It changes the look only: vision is the same.
- **Weather:** dust blows across the wasteland, ash drifts down over the city, and violet motes rise in crystal country. Off with Reduced visual effects.
- **Parked tanks look around:** idle turrets turn to scan now and then.
- **Smoother zoom:** units and buildings no longer shimmer when zoomed out.

## 2026-10-18 18:28 UTC

- **Save and continue:** **Save game** in the menu keeps your skirmish in this browser; **Continue** on the main menu picks it up where you left it.
- **Recent matches:** the main menu keeps the replays of your last five matches, ready to watch.
- **Rally warnings:** setting a rally point your ground units can't walk to (across water, on rock) tells you so.
- **Smart Override:** with several Halcyon units selected, Override goes out from the nearest one only; the others keep theirs for the next target.
- **Faction cursors:** amber crosshairs for the Holdout, cyan brackets for Halcyon, violet diamonds for the Choir.
- **Replays remember the camera:** reopen a replay and the view is where you left it.
- **Crash reports:** if the game hits an error, it sends an anonymous report so it can be fixed (see the privacy page).

## 2026-10-18 18:21 UTC

- **Units talk back:** select or order your units and they answer, in your faction's voice: Holdout troops on the radio ("Moving out", "Open fire!"), Halcyon machines ("Executing", "Target acquired"), the Choir in chorus ("We flow", "Silence them"). Replies are short and never pile up; turn them off with **Unit voices** in the menu.
- **Subtitles** for the announcer, under the battlefield.
- **Color-blind team colors:** a palette whose colors stay apart for every common color blindness.
- **Interface size:** scale the sidebar, top bar and command panel from 80% to 140%.

## 2026-10-18 18:14 UTC

- **Weapon ranges:** a selected defense shows how far it shoots, and so does one you are placing. Hold <kbd>Alt</kbd> to see the range of everything you have selected.
- **Health at a glance:** selection rings turn yellow and red as units get hurt, and hovering an enemy outlines it in red.
- **Veterans stand out:** rank chevrons are bigger, and a selected veteran shows its rank (Veteran, Elite, Heroic).
- **Event log:** the **Log** button in the top bar lists the last 20 events (attacks, losses, completions). Click one to look there.
- **Control groups** show the main unit type in each group.
- **Ability recharge** drains as a bar on the ability button.
- **Minimap:** buildings show their real size, units are sized by class (infantry small, heavy vehicles big), and Echo fields show in cyan.
- **Sell and repair:** in sell mode, hovering a building shows what it sells for; in repair mode, what a full repair costs.
- **Unit cards** list the research that improves the unit, ticked when done.
- **Pause menu briefing:** how to win, tips for your faction and the main keys, beside the settings.
- **Loading screen** with key art, who fights whom and a tip.
- **Skirmish setup:** each AI difficulty explains what it does, and three presets (Quick match, Long game, Rush) set up the map in one click.
- **Match report** shows actions per minute for each player.
- **Codex search:** type a unit or building name to find it, with what it is weak to and which units of each faction beat it.
- **Credits** are linked from the front page.

## 2026-10-18 15:39 UTC

## 2026-10-17 23:23 UTC

- **Smoother play:** scenery far off screen is no longer drawn, and the ground is cheaper to draw. Frame rate holds up much better when armies are on the move, especially on bigger maps.
- **Graphics setting:** in Settings, choose **High** (everything), **Balanced** (no small ground clutter or scenery shadows) or **Performance** (also renders at lower resolution with simpler ground) for older or slower computers.
- **City streets:** big blocks of ruins are cut by narrow streets, so cities read as city blocks with lanes between them. Infantry and vehicles can use the new lanes as shortcuts.

## 2026-10-17 21:12 UTC

- **Echo fields** look like crystal pushed up through the ground: clusters lean, loose shards lie around them, sizes vary, and towering mother crystals rise from the heart of rich fields. The tallest go as the field is mined out.
- **Crystal country:** small rock outcrops with violet spires replace the scattered crystal specks, and loose stones are dark instead of pale.

## 2026-10-17 21:03 UTC

- **Rock formations:** rocky outcrops are now big landforms spanning several tiles instead of rows of tile-sized stones. Wasteland outcrops are piles of sun-baked sandstone with scrub at their foot; in crystal country, dark rock is split by tall glowing violet crystal spires.
- **Crystal country:** the ground is scattered with dark stones and pale alien succulents instead of pink crystal confetti, and cliff tops carry the odd crystal outcrop rather than rows of identical spikes.
- **Shell craters** are real scorched hollows with a raised lip, lit by the sun like the rest of the ground, instead of flat painted rings.
- **Open ground** has dry gullies and wind-cut ridges, so wide plains aren't flat anymore.

- **River fords** look like shallow water over gravel instead of a flat green strip.

## 2026-10-17 18:39 UTC

## 2026-10-16 14:46 UTC

- **Halcyon: Uplink.** Select a fork, Halcyon's engineer, and press <kbd>F</kbd>. For 15 seconds it carries a bubble of network coverage with it, so the units around it keep their faster fire, repair and Recompile in the enemy's base. It recharges for 90 seconds.
- **Halcyon: Jam**, the first support power. Once you have a scan, press <kbd>Q</kbd> (or the new button above the build tabs) and click the map. Enemy weapons in the area fire 20% slower for 5 seconds. It recharges for 90 seconds. Air-gapped Holdout is immune.
- **Choir: escapes on their own.** A badly hurt Choir unit under fire now phases out or blinks away by itself, for players and the AI alike. You no longer need perfect micro to play Choir.
- **Choir: Chorus.** Choir units harmonize: each Choir unit within 3 tiles adds 2% damage, up to +6%. Violet threads show who is singing together.
- **Choir: Awaken**, a support power from the Chord. Press <kbd>Q</kbd> and click an Echo field near your structures: two shard guardians rise from its crystals and fight for 20 seconds. It costs some of the field's crystals and recharges for 2½ minutes.
- **Energy weapons hit Choir shields harder again:** 160% damage (was 140%).
- **Easy and Normal AI** no longer use abilities or support powers, like a player who doesn't micro. Hard and Brutal use them all.

- **Holdout Mules** carry 700 Echo again, the same as other harvesters: salvage is Holdout's extra income now.

- **Normal AI is beatable again.** It had picked up Hard's economy (three harvesters per refinery and extra factories); it is back to two harvesters and one of each production building.
- **Announcer:** a voice line that never reports finishing can no longer silence the rest of the match.
- **Balance page:** now also shows Normal AI against Normal AI, the closest thing to players who don't use abilities.
