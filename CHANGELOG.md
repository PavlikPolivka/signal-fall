# What's new

## 2026-10-16 12:25 UTC

- **See your network.** Halcyon players always see their coverage as a faint cyan grid with a bright edge. Inside it, units fire faster and repair themselves.
- **See your refunds.** A unit lost inside the network shows "+$" where it fell as Recompile pays back part of its cost, with a coin sound.
- **See your scrap.** Holdout players see a glinting marker on every wreck worth salvaging, and "+$" when a Wrench collects it.
- **Clearer tips.** The first-match tips explain place-first building and queuing units by tapping their key.

## 2026-10-16 12:13 UTC

- **Faction balance.** Every match-up is now within a few points of even (Hard AI against Hard AI, 300 matches each).
- **Choir combat units cost 10% more**, and Graces deal 12 damage instead of 14.
- **Bullets hit Choir shields harder:** 85% damage, up from 70%.
- **Energy weapons are softer on shields and aircraft:** 140% damage to shields (was 180%), and 50% to aircraft (was 75%), the same as bullets.
- **Halcyon lances** deal 75 damage, up from 65.
- **Holdout Mules** carry 800 Echo a trip.

## 2026-10-16 10:36 UTC

- **Richer economy.** Echo fields are bigger and regrow faster, so expanding to new fields pays off.
- **Refineries belong at new fields.** A refinery now costs more than a harvester, so build it beside a different field. On the same field, another harvester is the better buy.
- **Holdout Mules haul more:** 900 Echo a trip instead of 700.
- **Choir far seeds.** The Choir grows freely on Echo near its own structures. To grow on a far field, one of your units must stand near the spot. The new structure starts weak and grows to full strength, so guard it while it grows.
- **Smarter AI.** Each match it picks a strategy (rush, balanced, tech, turtle or raider), expands with construction vehicles, places refineries by fields, and hunts down the last enemy units instead of stalling.
- **The AI spends its money.** When credits pile up it builds more barracks and factories, it keeps teching up even when rushing, and it no longer fills its army with unarmed repair trucks.
- **Main menu.** The links fit on one line, and both big buttons are the same size.
- **Codex: building rules.** How each faction may build, how to expand, and what refineries are for.
- **Codex: Differences.** The three factions side by side, with examples from real matches and tips for each match-up.
- **What's new.** These notes, in the main menu.
- **Balance page.** Live results from the automatic balance checks, in the Codex: win rates, economy, favorite units and how each AI strategy fares.
- **New skirmish** on the end screen opens the skirmish setup again.
