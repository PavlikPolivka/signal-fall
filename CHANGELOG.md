# What's new

## 2026-10-17 08:47 UTC

- **Real water:** lakes and rivers now have depth (dark in the middle, shallow at the edges), a foam line along the shore and glints on the surface. They used to be a flat dark smear.
- **Rough ground shows up:** gravel and rubble patches were missing from every map because of a drawing bug. Maps now have them again.
- **New map props:** photo-scanned boulders, dead trees, bushes, barrels, crates, wrecks, street lamps and road barriers (from Poly Haven), plus small clutter scattered over open ground.
- **Echo fields look like fields:** crystals grow in clumps of different shapes and sizes, gently shimmer, and the ground under a field is stained teal with faint glowing veins. They no longer look like a carpet of identical cones.

## 2026-10-16 21:10 UTC

- **Buildings burn:** damaged buildings smoke, and badly damaged ones catch fire. More damage, more fire.
- **New models** for the Holdout construction yard (a warehouse instead of a bare crane frame), the Halcyon vehicle fabricator (a real hangar instead of two lines) and the Holdout Flak Track (now with its anti-aircraft turret).

## 2026-10-16 20:22 UTC

- **Crystal Choir soldiers:** Graces, Staccatos and Cantors are now violet crystal beings with glowing cores, carrying their team color on their shards.

## 2026-10-16 19:41 UTC

- **Grunts fixed:** no more hands stuck to their legs, and rifles no longer cross their faces. Soldiers hold their weapons pointing forward.

## 2026-10-16 19:15 UTC

- **Click what you see:** units are clickable anywhere on their new, bigger models.
- **New portraits** in the sidebar, the selection panel and the Codex, rendered from the new models.
- **Choir soldiers** show their team color.
- **New screenshots** on the landing page.

## 2026-10-16 19:01 UTC

- **All-new 3D models.** Every vehicle, building and soldier of all three factions has been rebuilt as a detailed 3D model: welded Holdout tanks, trucks and diesel works, white Halcyon hover machines and reactors, violet Choir crystals and geodes.
- **Animated soldiers:** infantry now really walk, aim their weapons and fall, in all eight directions, with team colors on their vests and armor.

## 2026-10-16 16:06 UTC

- **Comeback: rebuild from your refinery.** Lost your construction yard and every construction vehicle? As long as a refinery stands, it can build a new construction vehicle (Vehicles tab, normal price), even without a factory. The AI plays by the same rule.
- **Normal AI opens gentler:** it waits until minute 3 for its second refinery (Easy until minute 5), so a new player can keep up. Hard is unchanged.
- **New tip:** build a second refinery by another Echo field, and keep two or three harvesters per refinery.

## 2026-10-16 15:50 UTC

- **New terrain.** Real photo textures for every biome: dry earth and sandy gravel in the wasteland, worn concrete, asphalt and rubble in the city, violet-tinted rock in the crystal fields. They are graded darker than the units so the fighting stays readable, and stay crisp when you zoom out.

## 2026-10-16 15:44 UTC

- **Idle counters** in the top bar: "2 idle harvesters" (click, or <kbd>E</kbd>, to jump to one) and your idle army (click to select it all and look).
- **Production at a glance:** each sidebar tab shows how many items are queued, and a pulsing dot when its barracks or factory stands idle.
- **Low power you can see:** buildings that need power go dim and blink a red bolt.
- **Fog ghosts:** enemy buildings out of sight show as faded grey ghosts where you last saw them, with no health bar. You learn one is gone only by looking again.
- **Hold <kbd>Alt</kbd>** to show every health bar.

## 2026-10-16 14:46 UTC

- **Halcyon: Uplink.** Select a fork, Halcyon's engineer, and press <kbd>F</kbd>. For 15 seconds it carries a bubble of network coverage with it, so the units around it keep their faster fire, repair and Recompile in the enemy's base. It recharges for 90 seconds.
- **Halcyon: Jam**, the first support power. Once you have a scan, press <kbd>Q</kbd> (or the new button above the build tabs) and click the map. Enemy weapons in the area fire 20% slower for 5 seconds. It recharges for 90 seconds. Air-gapped Holdout is immune.
- **Choir: escapes on their own.** A badly hurt Choir unit under fire now phases out or blinks away by itself, for players and the AI alike. You no longer need perfect micro to play Choir.
- **Choir: Chorus.** Choir units harmonize: each Choir unit within 3 tiles adds 2% damage, up to +6%. Violet threads show who is singing together.
- **Choir: Awaken**, a support power from the Chord. Press <kbd>Q</kbd> and click an Echo field near your structures: two shard guardians rise from its crystals and fight for 20 seconds. It costs some of the field's crystals and recharges for 2½ minutes.
- **Energy weapons hit Choir shields harder again:** 160% damage (was 140%).
- **Easy and Normal AI** no longer use abilities or support powers, like a player who doesn't micro. Hard and Brutal use them all.

## 2026-10-16 13:19 UTC

- **Holdout: Dig In.** Select Grunts or Tankbusters and press <kbd>F</kbd>. They entrench behind sandbags and take 30% less damage and shoot 1 tile farther, but cannot move. Any order packs them up again.
- **Holdout: salvage on the move.** Any Holdout vehicle that drives over a wreck collects it, with no Wrench or order needed. A wreck pays 15% of the unit's cost (was 25%), since it now adds up over a match.
- **Holdout Mules** carry 700 Echo again, the same as other harvesters: salvage is Holdout's extra income now.
- **Air-gapped, reworked.** Halcyon's Override now works on Holdout machines, but only holds them half as long. Before, both sides' signature tricks cancelled out.
- **Normal AI is beatable again.** It had picked up Hard's economy (three harvesters per refinery and extra factories); it is back to two harvesters and one of each production building.
- **Announcer:** a voice line that never reports finishing can no longer silence the rest of the match.
- **Balance page:** now also shows Normal AI against Normal AI, the closest thing to players who don't use abilities.

## 2026-10-16 12:25 UTC

- **See your network.** Halcyon players always see their coverage as a faint cyan grid with a bright edge. Inside it, units fire faster and repair themselves.
- **See your refunds.** A unit lost inside the network shows "+$" where it fell as Recompile pays back part of its cost, with a coin sound.
- **See your scrap.** Holdout players see a glinting marker on every wreck worth salvaging, and "+$" when a Wrench collects it.
- **Clearer tips.** The first-match tips explain place-first building and queuing units by tapping their key.

## 2026-10-16 12:13 UTC

- **Faction balance.** Every match-up is now within a few points of even (Hard AI against Hard AI, 300 matches each).
- **Choir combat units cost 10% more**, and Graces deal 12 damage instead of 14.
- **Bullets hit Choir shields harder:** 85% damage, up from 70%.
- **Energy weapons are softer on shields and aircraft:** 140% damage to shields (was 180%), and 50% to aircraft (was 75%), the same as bullets.
- **Halcyon lances** deal 75 damage, up from 65.
- **Holdout Mules** carry 800 Echo a trip.

## 2026-10-16 10:36 UTC

- **Richer economy.** Echo fields are bigger and regrow faster, so expanding to new fields pays off.
- **Refineries belong at new fields.** A refinery now costs more than a harvester, so build it beside a different field. On the same field, another harvester is the better buy.
- **Holdout Mules haul more:** 900 Echo a trip instead of 700.
- **Choir far seeds.** The Choir grows freely on Echo near its own structures. To grow on a far field, one of your units must stand near the spot. The new structure starts weak and grows to full strength, so guard it while it grows.
- **Smarter AI.** Each match it picks a strategy (rush, balanced, tech, turtle or raider), expands with construction vehicles, places refineries by fields, and hunts down the last enemy units instead of stalling.
- **The AI spends its money.** When credits pile up it builds more barracks and factories, it keeps teching up even when rushing, and it no longer fills its army with unarmed repair trucks.
- **Main menu.** The links fit on one line, and both big buttons are the same size.
- **Codex: building rules.** How each faction may build, how to expand, and what refineries are for.
- **Codex: Differences.** The three factions side by side, with examples from real matches and tips for each match-up.
- **What's new.** These notes, in the main menu.
- **Balance page.** Live results from the automatic balance checks, in the Codex: win rates, economy, favorite units and how each AI strategy fares.
- **New skirmish** on the end screen opens the skirmish setup again.
